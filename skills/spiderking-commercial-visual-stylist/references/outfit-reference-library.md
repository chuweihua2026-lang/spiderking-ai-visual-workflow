## Retrieval Order

1. Product category and visible function.
2. Consumer gender, age, identity, and purchasing power.
3. Season, weather, and approved scene.
4. Required silhouette and shoe visibility.
5. Color and material compatibility.
6. Bag and accessory needs.

Shortlist two to five boards per output. Prefer functional fit and useful visual difference over category size or fashion novelty.

## Usage Boundary

Every board has `reference_only` status. Extract only reusable logic:

- garment silhouette and proportion;
- layering order;
- material relationships;
- 60/30/10 color structure;
- bag type and carrying logic;
- accessory hierarchy;
- broad season and scene suitability.

Never copy the reference person, face, pose, text, logo, branded item, distinctive print, background, reference shoe, or complete outfit. Never paste a board into final imagery.

## Diversity Gate

For the main poster and three scene images, record each selected asset ID. Across the three 9:16 scenes:

- do not reuse an outfit reference ID;
- do not repeat the same bottom silhouette or outerwear structure;
- vary color emphasis, materials, bag, accessories, hair or headwear;
- keep every look aligned with the same approved product and consumer profile.

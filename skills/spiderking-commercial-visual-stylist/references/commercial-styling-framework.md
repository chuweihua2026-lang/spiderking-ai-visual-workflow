# Commercial Styling Framework

## Role Standard

Operate as a senior international fashion stylist and visual director with more than 15 years of commercial campaign experience. The goal is not merely to create an attractive model. Create a professionally styled commercial model who fits the brand, clarifies the product position, and improves purchase intent.

## 1. Person Analysis

Record gender, age, height and body proportion, body characteristics, face and temperament, hairstyle, skin tone, and plausible role identity.

Choose one primary temperament:

- A, Youthful Energy: young, clean, sunny, campus-oriented; supports sneakers, denim, sweatshirts, and casual trousers.
- B, Urban Fashion: refined, trend-aware, premium; supports designed garments, fashion footwear, and distinctive accessories.
- C, Mature Business: professional, reliable, quality-led; supports tailoring, shirts, leather shoes, and watches.
- D, Outdoor Explorer: natural, free, functional; supports shell jackets, utility or technical trousers, and outdoor footwear.

## 2. Brand Positioning

Choose a primary and optional secondary direction:

- Premium Business: black, charcoal, navy, coffee, beige; leather, metal, and premium textiles.
- Young Trend: denim, contrast colors, sport and street elements.
- Outdoor Sport: earth tones, military green, and functional textiles.
- Women Fashion: soft colors, sophisticated gray, cream, and muted colors.

Do not force a single category when the product has a credible hybrid position. Record the priority order, such as `outdoor function first, urban gorpcore second`.

## 3. Product Analysis

For footwear, analyze shoe type, silhouette, color blocking, visible materials, sport attributes, business attributes, outdoor attributes, outsole geometry, upper protection, closure, and fashion language.

For bags, analyze bag shape, color, surface material, hardware color, carrying method, and use context. For luggage, distinguish business travel, student travel, and outdoor travel.

Keep the product as the visual center. Do not let clothing, props, typography, or model beauty overpower it. Distinguish confirmed visible evidence from inferred benefits and list unsupported claims explicitly.

## 4. Clothing Logic

Analyze top length, silhouette, shoulder line, material, and color. Balance proportions:

- Pair a loose top with a fitted or controlled bottom.
- A fitted top may support a wider trouser silhouette.
- Avoid an uncontrolled oversized top and oversized bottom combination.

Choose bottoms from the actual product category:

- Sneakers: denim, track pants, utility trousers, or product-appropriate fashion bottoms.
- Leather shoes: tailored trousers, straight trousers, or business trousers.
- Outdoor footwear: hiking trousers, technical trousers, cuffed cargo trousers, or performance leggings/yoga pants when the activity and silhouette support them.

For a three-image outdoor footwear set, use at least two long-bottom looks and three different bottom silhouettes. Do not default repeatedly to short skirts or shorts.

## 5. Footwear-Led Styling

The shoe is not selected last; it determines the outfit.

- Sneakers support youth, leisure, and trend styling.
- Business leather shoes support mature, premium, and professional styling.
- Outdoor shoes support exploration, travel, nature, utility dressing, trail styling, and urban outdoor/gorpcore styling.

Validate style unity, color echo, scene plausibility, and clear visibility. Avoid formal tailoring with a generic running shoe unless the concept explicitly requires a controlled fashion clash.

## 6. Bag System

Unify bag, clothing, footwear, and scene.

- Business bags support tailoring, shirts, and leather footwear.
- Women aged 18-25 generally support smaller, youthful, trend-led bags; ages 25-40 support refined quality-led bags; ages 40+ support mature and elegant bags.
- Outdoor looks support hydration packs, trail backpacks, technical crossbody bags, utility waist bags, or compact sling bags according to activity.

Choose a different bag type for each image in a multi-image campaign unless repetition is intentional.

## 7. Accessories

Select from watches, sunglasses, hats, belts, necklaces, earrings, bracelets, brooches, carabiners, scarf ties, bag charms, keychains, and phone charms. Keep accessories restrained but complete.

- Business menswear aligns leather shoes, belt, and watch materials.
- Womenswear creates an intentional echo among bag, shoes, jewelry, and hair detail.
- Outdoor styling may use a sports watch, sun visor or bucket hat, carabiner detail, functional pouch, and one or two personalized charms.

## 8. Color Algorithm

Use 60 percent main color, 30 percent support color, and 10 percent accent color. Keep the outfit to no more than three main colors unless a documented campaign concept requires more.

- Same-family colors feel premium and stable.
- Analogous colors feel natural and coordinated.
- Controlled contrast feels youthful and fashionable.
- Black, white, and gray support a commercial premium mood.

Let the product accent color occupy the 10 percent highlight role when that improves product focus.

## 9. Material Compatibility

Coordinate material language:

- Leather shoes, leather bag, and leather belt create premium unity.
- Sports footwear, sportswear, and functional textiles create athletic unity.
- Outdoor footwear, technical shell fabric, ripstop, breathable knit, webbing, and matte utility hardware create outdoor unity.

Reject obvious material conflict, such as cheap plastic styling beside premium business tailoring.

## 10. Pose Design

Design actions around the product:

- Shoes: show walking intent, leg line, side profile, outsole stance, or stable footing.
- Bags: show correct hand carry, shoulder carry, crossbody placement, and bag angle.
- Luggage: show pulling action and travel context.

For SpiderKing model scenes, keep the full shoe visible and uncropped. Do not let trouser hems, grass, foreground rocks, bags, props, motion blur, or shadows obscure the product.

## 11. Commercial Photography

Aim for international brand campaign quality with realistic skin, commercial lighting, editorial texture, accurate product scale, clear product detail, and intentional space for typography. Create an original SpiderKing campaign rather than copying another brand's recognizable campaign.

## 12. Final Audit

Before generation, verify:

- Person age and temperament fit.
- Brand positioning and product category fit.
- Outfit is unified and body proportions are controlled.
- Shoe matches clothing and scene.
- Bag use is correct and does not compete with the product.
- Accessories enrich the character without clutter.
- No more than three main colors; 60/30/10 balance is credible.
- Materials are compatible.
- Product is fully visible and proportionally accurate.
- The result has clear purchase intent and feels like a real brand advertisement.

Do not approve the generation brief until every applicable item passes.
